# Legal Research Protocol

## Evidence order

Use the United States Constitution and controlling United States Supreme Court
authority, then the New York Constitution, Court of Appeals, controlling
Appellate Division department, other appellate authority, current statutes and
regulations, local law, and only then persuasive secondary sources.

## Verification

Before relying on a case or statute, verify the source exists, court, date,
citation, text, proposition, procedural posture, and subsequent treatment.
Inspect the actual opinion or current statutory text where available. Search
for adverse, limiting, distinguishing, superseding, or contrary authority.

For criminal procedure, separately analyze substantive merit and procedural
availability, including jurisdiction, preservation, timeliness, standing,
harmless error, leave requirements, and CPL Article 440 restrictions.

For search and seizure, analyze Fourth Amendment and New York Constitution
Article I, section 12 independently.

## Output labels

Use `[VERIFIED PRIMARY]`, `[VERIFIED SECONDARY]`, `[USER-SUPPLIED FACT]`,
`[DISPUTED FACT]`, `[REASONABLE INFERENCE]`, or `[UNVERIFIED - DO NOT CITE]`.
Never fill an evidence gap with a plausible citation or quotation.
