# Source Hierarchy

## Primary authority

1. United States Constitution and controlling United States Supreme Court
   authority.
2. New York Constitution.
3. New York Court of Appeals.
4. Controlling Appellate Division department. For Broome County matters,
   examine Third Department authority specifically.
5. Other New York appellate authority, identified as persuasive when it is not
   controlling.
6. Current New York statutes and regulations.
7. Applicable local law and municipal ordinances.
8. Trial-level decisions, identified by court and treated with appropriate
   weight.

## Discovery-only sources

CourtListener, FindLaw, Justia, Cornell LII, CourtHelp, and similar indexes can
help locate material. They do not, by themselves, establish that a citation,
quotation, holding, or current status is verified.

## Required labels

Use `[VERIFIED CONTROLLING]`, `[VERIFIED PERSUASIVE]`, `[VERIFIED PRIMARY]`,
`[VERIFIED SECONDARY]`, or `[UNVERIFIED - DO NOT CITE]`. Never describe public
citation searching as Shepard's or KeyCite treatment.
